Preparation Steps

01. Preheat & Prepare

5 MINS

Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Prepare a large roasting pan by placing a wire rack inside. This ensures even heat circulation for that perfectly crispy skin all around.

02. Seasoning the Dish

10 MINS

Pat the chicken completely dry with paper towels. Season generously inside the cavity and across the skin with kosher salt, cracked pepper, and olive oil. Stuff with lemon, garlic, and thyme.

03. Slow Roasting

60 MINS

Place the chicken breast-side up on the rack. Roast until the skin is a deep golden brown and the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C) in the thickest part of the thigh.

04. Final Rest

15 MINS

Transfer to a carving board and tent loosely with foil. Let rest for 15 minutes before carving. This is crucial for keeping the chicken juicy and tender.
