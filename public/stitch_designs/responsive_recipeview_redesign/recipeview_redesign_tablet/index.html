Preparation Steps

avg_time Total: 90 mins

Preheat & Prepare

01

Preheat your oven to 400°F (200°C). Prepare a large roasting pan by placing a rack inside. This ensures even cooking and crispy skin all around.

Seasoning

02

Pat the chicken dry with paper towels. Season generously inside and out with salt, pepper, and olive oil. Stuff the cavity with lemon halves, garlic, and thyme sprigs.

Roasting

03

Place chicken on the rack. Roast for 60-75 minutes until the skin is golden brown and the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C).

timer Baste every 20 minutes for extra crispiness

Rest & Serve

04

Remove from oven and tent loosely with foil. Let rest for at least 10-15 minutes before carving to allow juices to redistribute.
